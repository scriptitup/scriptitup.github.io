Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Script It Up is a small software development studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the limited information we handle. This policy explains what we collect through our contact form and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the following information that you choose to provide:

  • Name
  • Email
  • Phone (optional)
  • Service
  • Budget
  • Message

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects with you. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

How we process your messages

Messages submitted through our contact form are transmitted to and processed securely through our backend services. We use these services to receive, store, and follow up on project inquiries.

Infrastructure providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Depending on the feature being used, your request may also be processed by infrastructure providers such as GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Workers, Render, Supabase, or other similar services. These providers process requests as part of delivering the service, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as necessary to respond to your message and to follow up on potential work. If we are not working together, we delete or anonymize the information once the conversation ends.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will respond to reasonable requests within a few business days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices evolve. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, email us at hello@scriptitup.io.
